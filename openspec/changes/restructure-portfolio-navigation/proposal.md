# Proposal

## Why

Film and Art need a clearer route from each practice to its areas and individual projects, with imagery that lets visitors explore Lauren's work in detail.

## What Changes

- Make Film and Art landing pages with large curated working-photo hero carousels and linked subsection carousels.
- Start with Filmography, On-set, and Scenic, Props & Sets for Film, and Inverted Paintings, Illustration, Jewellery, and Painting for Art. Editors can add, edit and reorder subsections in the CMS.
- Give each subsection a text-only introductory hero followed by project previews with curated carousels; give each project a text-only hero followed by photos.
- Add image detail exploration: holding a photo enlarges it within the photo area and movement reveals details; provide equivalent tap and keyboard controls.
- Preserve existing published URLs or provide tested redirects; retain approved content and enquiry controls. Use only media cleared for the exact use. Represent missing material honestly and use synthetic fixtures for development.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `portfolio-browsing`: Section hierarchy, curated galleries, accessible carousels and image-detail exploration.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing publication safeguards remain unchanged.

## Impact

Film and Art routes, navigation, templates, content relationships and editorial configuration may change. Reuse the current Astro components, content contracts and Storybook. No new service is assumed. Implementing this proposal requires a separate request. Image selection, factual copy and permission for each new use require their own approval; deferred migration is not resumed by this proposal. Deployment, DNS, external messages and launch remain outside scope.
