A creative with a fine arts background and a decade working in the film industry as a Standby Painter, Lead Props Painter, and Art Assistant. Known for my strong work ethic, keen eye for visual detail, and hands-on practical skills, I bring both precision and adaptability to every project. I enjoy planning, problem-solving and the diversity of the Art Department. Friendly and collaborative by nature, I'm a calm & solutions-focused presence - whether in the workshop, on set, or on location. You can view my work and full filmography on my website.
