My painting practice explores the materiality of paint itself, existing at the intersection of abstraction and representation. I’m drawn to the tension between building up and breaking down, capturing a sense of flux and transformation on the surface. I investigate themes of human impact on the environment, often evoking architectural forms and geometric structures. My work reflects a constant state of transition, where solid shapes and fragmented textures coexist, inviting viewers to consider the balance between creation and decay.

Acrylic on paper illustrations of city streets and reflections in windows.
