A lot of projects I can't show you - but he's a sneak peak behind the scenes into some of the films I have worked on.

As an on-set painter, I work both in studio and out on location—through sun, rain, long days, and overnight shoots. The work is always changing: sometimes I'm up ladders or on boats, sometimes I'm focused on tiny, details, and other times large scale sets. While I mainly use paint, I often work with blood, mud, and other materials to meet the needs of the scene. It’s a fast-paced, unpredictable environment, and I love the challenge of making each moment on screen look real and alive.
