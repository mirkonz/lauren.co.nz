## Proposed Oil Painting Landscapes collection

**Title:** Oil Painting Landscapes

**Introduction:** Landscapes and cityscapes exploring light.

This short introduction is edited from the [draft's](../src/content/art-collections/oil-painting-landscapes/index.md) “Landscapes and city scapes looking at light, oil on canvas.” Confirm the title and introduction. I recommend omitting the separate “oil on canvas” medium field until its scope across the selected works is confirmed; no individual artwork medium or dimension is inferred from the collection title. The longer practice-wide statement already used on Artificial Landscapes is not repeated here.

**Proposed images, in order:** L02, L01, L03, L04, L05, L06. Lead with L02. This keeps the initial selection focused on road, city-light and countryside views. L07–L09 remain alternatives; none is deleted or assigned to a different collection.
